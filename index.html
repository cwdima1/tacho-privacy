Full policy

Summary of what the app does with data

  • Driving and rest records (shift times, activities, counters) are stored locally on your device. No account is required to use the app.
  • Cloud backup: the app automatically backs up its current timer state under a random device UUID (not linked to your name or email) to our processor Supabase (EU, Frankfurt), so it can be restored after a reinstall. Deletion on request.
  • Optional connection to a fleet / friends feature: if you link the app to a Tachora account, your driving status and (for friends) your last position are shared with your employer or the friends you accept.
  • Device permissions (all optional, revocable in system settings): location (detecting driving/stopping and the current country; in the background while a shift is open), Bluetooth (connection to the vehicle tachograph, read on your device only), camera (scanning QR codes), notifications.
  • Push notifications: device tokens via Apple Push Notification Service / Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Advertising: the free app shows ads, including through Google AdMob. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent (Google consent dialog; on iOS also Apple's tracking prompt). You can change your choice in the app settings.
  • Error reports: crash logs are processed by Sentry.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and object to processing, and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Contact: support@tachora.app.
